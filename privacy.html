To request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held by the App, email notsimondean@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
